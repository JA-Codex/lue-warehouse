## Story

As management, I want clear findings with evidence and recommended actions, so that I can decide.

## What was done

Wrote `etl/reports/insights.py`, which builds the Business Insights Report PDF (8 pages, submitted in the Phase 3 zip): a summary
with the portfolio figures (billed PKR 5,676,652,818, collected PKR 5,347,289,709, collection rate 94.2%, arrears
PKR 329,363,109, occupancy 84.5%, cash received up 8.2% in FY2024-25) and a table of the four findings and decisions,
a page on monthly revenue with growth and the ROLLUP and CUBE results, then one section per insight with its chart, decision and caveat, the Executive Overview, Detailed Analysis and
Decision Page screenshots, and notes on the synthetic data. The report needs no database; each figure is checked
against the evidence file it comes from. The earlier `--insights` section of `etl/reports/build.py`, whose figures did
not come from the warehouse, was removed; `build.py --insights` now calls the new script.
