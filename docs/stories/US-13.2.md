## Story

As an executive, I want the key numbers and trend at a glance, so that I know how the portfolio is performing.

## What was done

Built in Power BI Desktop on the team's model (PR #27), following `docs/powerbi/DASHBOARD_GUIDE.md` Part 2: three
slicers synced to all pages (fiscal year, city, building class); four KPI cards (cash received with the year before,
collection rate with billed, occupancy with active leases, arrears with the arrears ratio); cash received by month
against the year before; billed and the collection rate by month; occupancy by month; the ten buildings with the highest
arrears ratio, which is where the drill-through starts.
