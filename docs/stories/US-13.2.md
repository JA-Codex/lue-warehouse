## Story

As an executive, I want the key numbers and trend at a glance, so that I know how the portfolio is performing.

## The page

[`docs/powerbi/README.md`](../powerbi/README.md), step 8: four KPI cards (cash received, the brief's revenue
analogue, with its change against the year before; collection rate; occupancy; arrears with the arrears ratio),
cash received by month against the year before (SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR on the marked date table; blank where the year
before is outside the data), billed and the collection rate by month, arrears ratio by building; slicers for fiscal
year, city and building class, synced to all three pages. The values the cards must show with no slicer, with
FY2024-25 and with Lahore are in [`../evidence/us-13.1/01_expected_values.txt`](../evidence/us-13.1/01_expected_values.txt)
(whole window: cash received PKR 5,347,289,709, collection rate 94.2 percent, occupancy 84.5 percent, arrears
PKR 329,363,109 at 5.8 percent of billed).
