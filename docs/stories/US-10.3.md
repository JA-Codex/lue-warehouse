## Story

As a data owner, I want proof that reports agree with the warehouse and that Power BI can only read, so that
decisions rest on trustworthy numbers.

## What was done

`scripts/test_reporting.py` (18 checks, reads only) compares every mart with queries written straight on the
facts, by another route (day by day from the facts and `dim_date`, not through the reporting views), in **every
month** and for every measure: billed, collected (cohort), cash, arrears at month end (recomputed day by day for
the first, middle and last month), unit-days, occupied days, month-end rent roll and arrears, active leases,
completed vacancy spells (counted as a vacant day followed by a let one), requests, resolution, SLA breaches,
costs, rent collected, NOI. It confirms the row counts, the unique grains and the aliases.

Access: `lue_reader` already had `USAGE` on `lue_reporting` only and default `SELECT` on what `lue_etl` creates
there (US-1.1). The test connects as `lue_reader` and shows it can read all 19 objects, and is refused the rest
with PostgreSQL's own messages: no access to `lue_dw`, `lue_staging` or `lue_audit` ("permission denied for
schema"), no write ("cannot execute DELETE in a read-only transaction"), no create even in a read-write
transaction ("permission denied for schema lue_reporting"), and no refresh (`refresh_marts()` is revoked from
`PUBLIC`: "permission denied for function").
