## Story

As an executive, I want a single page of prioritised recommendations backed by data so that I can make decisions without reading the full analysis.

## What was done

1. **Three recommendation cards** — each is a Power BI card or text box with a DAX dynamic-text measure that fills in the key number from the current slicer selection:
   - **R1 — Collections**: "Building [Top Arrears Building] carries [Arrears PKR] in outstanding arrears, [Arrears Ratio]% of billed rent.  Consider escalation before Q[Next Quarter]." (`Top Arrears Building` and `Arrears Ratio` are dynamic DAX measures.)
   - **R2 — Maintenance**: "Vendor [Top Breach Vendor] breached SLA on [SLA Breach %]% of tickets — above the [Trade] category average of [Category SLA Avg]%.  Review vendor contract or reassign critical categories."
   - **R3 — Leasing**: "Incentive offers reduced average vacancy by [Vacancy Reduction Days] days ([Incentive Effect %]% shorter). Extend the offer programme to cities where vacancy > [Vacancy Threshold] days."
2. **Supporting mini-visuals** — one small KPI card per recommendation showing the key measure, so the number is visible at a glance beside the text.
3. **Assumptions and risks box** — a plain text box (white background, left-aligned) listing:
   - Data is synthetic (generated by `scripts/generate.py`); real figures will differ.
   - Management fee is an 8% assumption (`US-10.2` decision D18).
   - Incentive effect is a population average across all offers; a controlled experiment would be needed to prove causation.
   - Vendor breach data covers only the tickets in the data window; vendors with fewer than 10 tickets are excluded from R2.
4. **Page layout** — 1280 × 720, three recommendation panels arranged vertically on the left (60 % width), mini-visuals on the right (38 % width), assumptions box at the bottom full-width.  Page name in Power BI: **"Decision"**.
5. **No new SQL** — all measures are already in `docs/powerbi/measures.dax`; the DAX dynamic-text measures reference `Top Arrears Building` (a `TOPN`-based measure) and `Top Breach Vendor` similarly.

## Decisions

- Three recommendations, not more, to keep the page readable in a single view without scrolling.
- The assumptions box is mandatory — the data is synthetic and any reader taking the recommendations at face value needs to know this.
- Dynamic text measures are used (not static text) so the page responds to the fiscal year and city slicers on the same page; a director looking at Lahore only sees Lahore's worst building.
