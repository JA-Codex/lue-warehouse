## Story

As management, I want an explicit recommendation backed by the dashboard, so that analysis ends in a decision.

## The recommendations

Each is a DAX measure (`D1 Text` to `D3 Text` in `docs/powerbi/measures.dax`) that picks its subject from the
current selection and states the figure, the action, the owner and the expected effect; the numbers stay in sync
with the slicers. On the full profile, with no slicer set:

1. **Arrears (D1)**: the building with the highest arrears ratio, GLB03 (Gulberg Residency 2): 21.8 percent against
   5.8 for the portfolio, and a collection rate of 78.2 percent, 16.0 points under the portfolio. Action: a weekly
   call list and payment plans for its chronically late tenants; owner: the finance head; effect: recovering half its
   PKR 64,267,854 of arrears cuts the portfolio's arrears by 10 percent (Q2, planted pattern 1).
2. **Maintenance (D2)**: the vendor slowest against its own trade (20 or more resolved requests), V-001 (Sheikh
   Plumbing Works): 3.1 days a request, 2.3 times the plumbing average, SLA breached on 86.5 percent of its jobs.
   Action: give its urgent jobs to the fastest plumber (V-006) and review its contract; owner: the maintenance manager;
   effect: about 76 fewer days of waiting over the window (Q9a, planted pattern 2). Vendors are compared within their
   trade because some trades take longer by nature (raw days would pick a civil-works vendor).
3. **Vacancy (D3)**: the city and unit type with the lowest occupancy (10 or more units), apartments in Karachi:
   78.2 percent against 84.5. Action: a vacancy incentive, marketed before the summer peak; owner: the leasing
   manager; effect: about 23 more units let, about PKR 1.8 million of rent a month (Q6a, Q13).

Beside each text goes its supporting visual, and the page has an assumptions and risks box (synthetic data, the
cohort rate's reading, the thresholds, the estimates); guide, step 10. `scripts/test_powerbi.py` checks that each
text picks what queries on the facts pick.
