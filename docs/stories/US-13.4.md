## Story

As management, I want an explicit recommendation backed by the dashboard, so that analysis ends in a decision.

## What was done

Built following `docs/powerbi/DASHBOARD_GUIDE.md` Part 4: three recommendations as DAX text measures that pick their
building, vendor and segment from the slicers, each beside its evidence chart, with an assumptions box and the data
window. With no slicer: collect the arrears at Gulberg Residency 2 (21.8% against 5.8%); move urgent plumbing work from
Sheikh Plumbing Works (2.3 times its trade's days); a vacancy incentive for Karachi apartments (78.2% against 84.5%).
A helper measure, `Days vs Trade (20+)`, ranks only vendors with 20 or more resolved requests on the vendor chart.
