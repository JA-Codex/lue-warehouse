## Story

As a marker, I want a clear before and after report, so that the optimisation work is easy to verify.

## What was done

`python etl/reports/indexing.py` builds `docs/reports/LUE_Indexing_Report.pdf` (7 pages) from the recorded run; every
number is read from it:

1. **Summary**: the comparison table (query, change, time and cost before and after, plan type before and after,
   verdict) and each query's run ranges and spread.
2. **The baseline**: the warehouse already had primary-key and unique-constraint indexes (D20), listed as measured.
3. **Method**: the harness, the interleaved rounds, the stated tolerance and why the machine needs it.
4. **Each experiment**: what changed, the measurement, why it worked or did not, and the plan before and after.
5. **What each index costs**: size, build time, load cost, the queries that use it.
6. **Recommendations**: keep the two date indexes and the occupancy index; drop the partial index; keep the partitions
   for maintenance; make Q7 fast with precomputed spells; what matters for Power BI.

The explanations are written once per query and per verdict; the generator refuses to build when a run measures a
verdict that its plans cannot explain, so the text cannot argue against the numbers. The stretch comparison (Q1 through
relational tables, a mart and a hybrid) was not done.
