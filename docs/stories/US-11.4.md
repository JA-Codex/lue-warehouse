## Story

As an executive, I want subtotals and grand totals in one result, so that I can see detail and summary together.

## What was done

In `sql/60_analytics/olap.sql`, on the collected amount (payments paid in the window):

1. **O1** `ROLLUP (region, city, building_code)` (the brief's Region, Category, Product): 40 buildings, 4 city and
   3 region subtotals and the grand total.
2. **O2** `CUBE (city, channel_group)` (the brief's Region by Channel): every city and channel, both margins and the
   grand total.
3. Every subtotal row is labelled from `GROUPING()` (`row_level`, "All cities", "All channels"), so a subtotal is
   never read as missing data.
4. **O3** verifies both: 8 checks over 23 subtotals (grand totals = the base total, every city and region subtotal =
   the sum of its parts, the CUBE's city totals = the ROLLUP's).
