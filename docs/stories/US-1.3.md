## Story

As a data engineer, I want the six fact tables created with their foreign keys and the occupancy fact partitioned
by month, so that loads are validated and large scans can be pruned.

## What was done

1. `sql/10_ddl/02_facts.sql`: lines 282-531 of the frozen `physical_model.sql` (the 6 facts, 41 foreign keys and
   48 monthly partitions of `fact_unit_occupancy_daily`), with four changes:
   - the 38 `CREATE INDEX` lines removed (D20 baseline);
   - `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`, so a re-run is safe;
   - the 4 fact surrogate keys `GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS IDENTITY` (D6);
   - a default partition, `fact_unit_occupancy_daily_default`, for dates outside July 2023 - June 2027.
2. `scripts/test_facts.py`: 24 acceptance checks.
3. `scripts/check_contract.py` now reports a SQL syntax error as a clear FAIL line naming the file.
4. `make db-test` also runs the fact tests.

## Decisions and deviations

- **No secondary indexes (D20).** Only primary-key and unique-constraint indexes exist. The pack's 38 indexes are
  the Phase 3 experiment (US-12.2), measured against this baseline.
- **Default partition added** (plan US-1.3 step 3; not in the pack). Rows outside the range are kept, not lost.
- **Fact surrogate keys are identity columns**, matching the dimensions (D6).
