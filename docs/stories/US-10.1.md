## Story

As an analyst, I want ready-made views over the star schemas, so that common reporting needs are one query away.

## What was done

All in `sql/50_reporting/01_reporting.sql`, deployed by `scripts/db_deploy.py` (and so by every pipeline run):

1. **`vw_monthly_collections`**, one row per month: billed (net amount of the lines due in the month), collected
   against those lines and the **cohort collection rate (D17)**, cash received (paid-month basis, a separate
   column), arrears at month end, and the growth of billed and cash against the previous month with `LAG`.
2. **`vw_tenant_analysis`**, one row per tenant version, so the segment is the one valid when the tenant paid (the
   fact's point-in-time key): payments, late payments and their share, amount-weighted days past due, total
   collected, current arrears, and a payer segment (On time, Occasionally late, Chronically late, No payments).
3. **`vw_unit_performance`**, one row per building and unit type: occupancy rate (occupied unit-days / unit-days),
   average vacancy days (pack KPI), average contract rent and current rent roll, maintenance cost per unit-month
   (pack KPI), days to resolve and SLA breach rate.
4. **The brief's names (D18)**: `vw_monthly_sales`, `vw_customer_analysis` and `vw_product_performance`, each
   `SELECT *` from its LUE view.
5. **Nine slicer views** `vw_dim_*`: date, month, building, unit (the location hierarchy), tenant (segment), owner,
   vendor, maintenance category, payment method; technical columns left out, keys kept for relationships.
6. **Every column documented**: [`docs/reporting.md`](../reporting.md) (185 columns, the measure definitions, the
   additivity rule); the README maps the brief's names. CI fails if a column is not documented
   (`scripts/test_reporting_contract.py`).

Two parameter views hold what the marts and views share: the data window (the occupancy snapshot's first and last
day) and the stated assumptions (an 8 percent management fee, the late-payer bands).
