## Story

As a manager, I want to drill into buildings, cities, tenants and vendors, so that I can find where problems sit.

## The page

[`docs/powerbi/README.md`](../powerbi/README.md), step 9: buildings and unit types (a ranked bar chart and a
city, building and unit type matrix: the brief's product analysis), cities (collection rate and occupancy by city:
regional analysis), tenant segmentation (tenants by payer segment, from `vw_tenant_analysis`, and a tenant table),
vendor performance (days to resolve, days against the vendor's own trade, SLA breach rate). Cross-filtering stays on.

**The drill-through from a building to its tenants** needs tenants by building, which no view had:
`vw_tenant_by_building` (one row per tenant version and building, over the window) was added to the reporting
layer. `scripts/test_powerbi.py` checks that it adds up to `vw_tenant_analysis` by tenant and to Q2's billed and
arrears by building (3,879 rows, 40 buildings on the full profile). The drill-through target is the Detailed
Analysis page itself (its *Drill through* field is the building name), so the dashboard keeps exactly three pages:
right-click a building on page 1 or 3, Drill through, and page 2 opens with that building's tenants.
