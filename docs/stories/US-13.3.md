## Story

As a manager, I want to drill into buildings, cities, tenants and vendors, so that I can find where problems sit.

## What was done

Built following `docs/powerbi/DASHBOARD_GUIDE.md` Part 3: a city and building matrix with colour-scaled occupancy,
collection rate and arrears ratio (the brief's product analysis), occupancy by unit type, collection rate and occupancy
by city (regional analysis), tenants by payer segment and a tenant table by arrears (segmentation, from
`vw_tenant_by_building`, added in US-13.1 for this drill-through), and vendors ranked against their own trade. The page
is the drill-through target on the building name, so the dashboard keeps exactly three pages.
