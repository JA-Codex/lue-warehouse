## Story

As a marker, I want the analytical work in clean, runnable files, so that every query can be verified quickly.

## What was done

- Window analytics: **W1** `RANK` and `DENSE_RANK` of buildings by arrears, **W2** `LAG` growth in collections,
  **W3** running totals within each fiscal year, **W4** a three-month moving average (`ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING`), the
  collection rate over the same months as a ratio of sums. Q1, Q2, Q5 to Q12 use `RANK` and `LAG` too.
- Consolidated in `sql/60_analytics/analytical.sql` (Q1 to Q14, W1 to W4) and `olap.sql` (O1 to O3); each query
  headed by its question number, grain, tables and definition (D17 and the rest). CI checks the headers, that every
  statement only reads, and that every query is answered in `docs/analytics.md` (`scripts/test_analytics_contract.py`).
- `scripts/run_analytics.py` runs each query with its rows and time; `scripts/test_analytics.py` (22 checks)
  checks the answers against the marts, the planted patterns and the OLAP verification.
- **Independent cross-checks with pandas**, from the facts: Q1 (every building and month), Q12 (every owner's NOI)
  and O1 (every ROLLUP row).
