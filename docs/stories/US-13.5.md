## How it is validated

`docs/powerbi/validation.dax` runs in Power BI's DAX query view and returns every numeric measure (44) for the whole
window, March 2025, FY2024-25 and Lahore, and the three decision texts per selection. The same table, computed from
the marts in SQL and checked against the facts, is in `docs/evidence/us-13.1/expected_measures.csv`; the side-by-side
table to fill is `docs/evidence/us-13.5/README.md`. Slicer combinations, tooltips and page load are checked as in
step 11 of the guide; the PBIX and the PDF of the three pages go in `powerbi/`.
