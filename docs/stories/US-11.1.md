## Story

As a finance head, I want collection, arrears and payment behaviour answered in SQL, so that I can act on them.

## What was done

In `sql/60_analytics/analytical.sql`, each query headed by its question, grain, tables and definition:

- **Q1** collection rate by building and month on the cohort basis (D17), with `LAG` for the change against the
  previous month. Portfolio 94.2 percent; GLB03 78.2 percent.
- **Q2** arrears ratio by building (arrears at the window end / billed), ranked: GLB03 21.8 percent, twice the next.
- **Q3** ageing of the overdue lines at the last month-end: the pack's buckets 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ (the plan's
  "61 and over" is the last two); 86 percent is over 90 days.
- **Q4** repeat late payers (3 or more payments over 15 days late in the last 12 months), with the employer sector of
  the tenant version that paid: 328 tenants; lateness from 8.4 percent (Government) to 14.4 (Manufacturing).
- **Q5** settlement speed by payment method (settlement lag from the S3 files, and days past due): Raast fastest.

Results and a one-line interpretation each: [`docs/analytics.md`](../analytics.md).
