## Story

As an operations head, I want occupancy, vacancy duration and renewal rates answered, so that I can target leasing
effort.

## What was done

- **Q6a** occupancy and vacancy by month, city and unit type: the monthly average of daily occupancy (occupied
  unit-days / unit-days). Karachi apartments are lowest (78.2 percent) and falling. **Q6b** new-tenant move-ins by
  month: June to August 1.43 times the other months (planted pattern 3).
- **Q7** vacancy duration by unit type, gaps and islands over consecutive vacant days. **Censoring rule** (beside
  the query): completed spells only; a spell that begins on the window's first day or is open on its last day is
  left out. Offices take longest (141 days on average).
- **Q8** renewal rate by building: renewed (a RENEW event on the successor lease) / leases that reached expiry.
  Karachi renews 17.9 percent against 36.1 elsewhere (planted pattern 6). A lease-link rate beside it shows what the
  quarantined RENEW events would add.
