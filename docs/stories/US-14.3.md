## Story

As presenters, we want a concise story and a reliable live demo, so that the work is communicated clearly under time pressure.

## What was done

listener who has not seen the project: the business at a glance and who needs answers; the design (six processes,

the 14 business questions with their answers; monthly revenue with growth; ROLLUP and CUBE results; three findings
with their charts; the indexing results; the dashboard; and the three decisions. Wrote the outline, a demo script on
the small profile, the timing, a fallback that needs no database, and answers to the five likely questions.
