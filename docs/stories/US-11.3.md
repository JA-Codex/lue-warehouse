## Story

As an operations and owner-relations team, I want maintenance performance, owner yield and external effects
answered, so that vendors and owners can be managed with evidence.

## What was done

- **Q9a** time to resolve by category and vendor (median against the SLA too): V-001 is the slowest plumber, 1.36
  times the SLA against 0.68 (planted pattern 2). **Q9b** by the vendor's rating at the time (SCD2): C-rated vendors
  are the slowest in every trade.
- **Q10** SLA breach rate by building; **Q11** maintenance cost per unit-month by building (the pack's KPI).
- **Q12** NOI per owner: rent collected (**charge type RENT only**) - actual maintenance cost - the management fee.
  **Fee assumption** (beside the query): 8 percent of rent collected, held in `lue_reporting.vw_reporting_parameter`.
- **Q13** incentives: completed vacancy spells with an offer against the comparison group with none: 57.5 against
  134.3 days, 0.43 times (planted pattern 4).
- **Q14** leak requests after heavy rain (**threshold**: 10.0 mm or more on the day or the day before): 6.3 times the
  rate of other days (planted pattern 5).
