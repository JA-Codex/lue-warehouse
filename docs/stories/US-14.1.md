## Story

As an analyst, I want a ranked list of well-evidenced insights, so that the report contains findings that matter.

## What was done

Read the answers to Q1 to Q14, W1 to W4 and the OLAP queries (`docs/analytics.md`, full profile) and the Decision
Page texts (`docs/evidence/us-13.1/decisions.txt`), sized each candidate in PKR or days and ranked them. Four insights
were kept: arrears at GLB03 (PKR 64,267,854), Karachi apartment vacancy and renewals (about PKR 1,775,190 of rent a
month), the slow plumbing vendor V-001 (about 76 days of waiting) and rain-driven leaks (6.3 times). All six planted
patterns are accounted for, three of them inside the leasing insight.
