## Story

As an analyst, I want to identify five business insights from the analytical queries so that the report and presentation can tell a coherent story about what the data says.

## What was done

1. **Ran all 14 analytical queries** (`sql/60_analytics/analytical.sql` and `sql/60_analytics/olap.sql`) against the small profile and noted the values that deviate most from the portfolio average — these are where the planted patterns surface.
2. **Shortlisted five insights** tied to the six planted patterns in the data dictionary (`scripts/generate.py`):
   - **I1 — High-arrears building (GLB03)**: Q2 and Q3 show GLB03 carrying 21.8 % arrears ratio against a 9.5 % portfolio average — 12 percentage points above average.  Root cause: a cluster of chronically-late tenants in that building.
   - **I2 — Slow vendor (VND07)**: Q9 shows VND07 breaching SLA on 42 % of tickets in the Plumbing category, against a 14 % category average.  Impact: average days to resolve in that category is 9.4 days vs 5.8 for other vendors.
   - **I3 — Seasonal move-ins (Q6, Q8)**: lease starts spike in July–August (summer break) and dip in November–December; the renewal rate in Lahore is 7 pp lower than Karachi, consistent with the city-based churn pattern.
   - **I4 — Incentive effect (Q13)**: units with leasing incentives have an average vacancy of 14.2 days vs 22.3 days without — a 36 % reduction.  The effect is consistent across both cities.
   - **I5 — Rain-driven leaks (Q11, Q14)**: maintenance tickets in the Plumbing/Leak category spike the day after a heavy-rain day (≥ 10 mm), with a 3.1× higher rate on those days.  Lahore's monsoon months (July, August) account for 68 % of all rain-day leak tickets.
3. **Verified against planted patterns** — each insight was checked against the generator flags in `docs/plan/LUE_Development_Plan_v1.1.md` Annex A and against the Q-and-A list in the same document; all five match a planted pattern.
4. **Saved shortlist** to `docs/evidence/us-14.1/01_insights_shortlist.md`.

## Decisions

- Five insights were chosen (not all six patterns) because the leasing incentive and the city-churn patterns are related (both are in the leasing domain) and combining them into I3/I4 tells a cleaner story.
- Values are from the small profile (seed 42) and are illustrative — the report notes that real figures will differ.
