# Planted business patterns

Profile `full`. Written by `etl/gen_defects.py` (US-2.6) from
`etl/patterns.py`.
The patterns are part of the clean data: a clean control batch has them too. Measure them
with `python scripts/check_patterns.py --data <the generated folder>`.

| # | Pattern | Where | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High-arrears building | building `GLB03` | overdue share of billed rent at least 2.0 times the rest of the portfolio's |
| 2 | Slow vendor | vendor `V-001` (Plumbing) | median resolve time over SLA at least 1.6 times that of the other vendors of the trade |
| 3 | Seasonal move-ins | months 6, 7, 8 | a peak month has at least 1.3 times the new-tenant move-ins of an average other month |
| 4 | Incentive shortens vacancy | offers in `s6/incentive_log.csv` | mean vacancy with an offer at most 0.75 of the mean vacancy without one |
| 5 | Rain-driven leak tickets | PLB-LEAK, CIV-SEEP, CIV-ROOF after 10.0 mm of rain or more | leak tickets per unit-day at least 3.0 times higher on a rainy day or the day after than on other days |
| 6 | Renewal churn | city `Karachi` | renewal rate at most 0.75 of the other cities' rate |

The small profile (2 buildings, 3 months) is too small to measure most effects; the
thresholds apply to the full profile.
