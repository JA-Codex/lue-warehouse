## Slide 2 — Business Problem

- LUE manages 2,000+ units across two cities; finance runs on spreadsheets
- Three questions management cannot answer today:
  - Which buildings are collecting rent reliably?
  - Which vendors are costing us the most?
  - When should we offer incentives to cut vacancy?
- **Goal:** a reproducible warehouse that answers these questions from the raw sources

---

## Slide 3 — Design Decisions (key five)

| Decision | What we chose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Kimball star schema | Additive facts, OLAP slicing |
| D3 | Synthetic data with planted defects | Safe to publish; controlled quality testing |
| D14 | Occupancy snapshot refreshes last 35 days | Avoids full rebuild; handles late arrivals |
| D17 | Collection rate = cohort (invoiced in period, paid by end) | Avoids cherry-picking cash timing |
| D20 | Candidate indexes excluded from deploy | Keeps baseline stable; measured separately |

---

## Slide 4 — Architecture

```
S1–S6 (sources) → Bronze (raw) → Validation (DQ-01–06)
  → Staging (cleaned) → Warehouse (dimensions + facts)
  → Reporting layer → Power BI dashboard
                    → PDF reports
```

- 6 sources, 11-step pipeline (`run_pipeline.py`)
- 7 dimensions, 5 facts, 4 marts
- 1 command to rebuild: `make rebuild`

---

## Slide 5 — ETL Pipeline

- **Bronze:** 6 sources, ~200 k rows total, landed unchanged
- **Validation:** 44 DQ rules (DQ-01 to DQ-06); quarantine for rejects
- **Staging:** type-cast, deduplicated, derived fields; SCD2 for 4 dimensions
- **Facts:** 5 fact tables; grain enforced by delete-update-insert sync
- **Reconciliation:** DQ-07 — source = warehouse + quarantined for every measure-month
- **Pipeline run log:** every step tracked; non-critical failures do not stop the run

## Slide 8 — Performance

- 14 analytical queries; 7 improved with candidate indexes (D20 excluded from deploy)
- Benchmark harness: 3 back-to-back serial runs; best of 3 kept
- Largest gain: Q2 arrears query — 820 ms → 95 ms (8.6× faster) with a building+date index
- Baseline kept in `sql/70_performance/`; `make benchmark` reruns against the live database

## Slide 11 — Lessons Learned

- Synthetic data with planted patterns makes quality testing tractable — real sources need the same approach
- Decision log (D1–D20) prevented scope creep; every contested decision has a reason
- Offline contract tests (no database) catch structural breaks in CI without a full pipeline run
- Power BI model validation in Python (DAX → SQL equivalence) found two measure bugs before the demo

---

## Slide 12 — What is Not Done (and Why)

- No live Airflow / dbt orchestration (D2 — out of scope for Phase 2)
- Candidate indexes excluded from deploy (D20 — baseline stability)
- Real credential management (secrets manager) — not needed for a local demo database
